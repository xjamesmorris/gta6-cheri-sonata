Title: Right plays story mode · Down changes artwork
Arrow keys: drive · Space / Enter: press
Car hits build yellow heat into red stars. Green flashing means you are escaping.
Denser traffic includes trucks, buses, and trailers. Police arrive at three stars and aim only once per pass.
Police collisions are fatal at five. Police can explode when they hit solid objects.
R2 flashes green for cash/small gold, gold for large gold, and orange for damage.
WASTED holds red for 3 seconds, then fades to monochrome over 6 seconds as both RGB LEDs turn purple and dim to off.
Press after WASTED for the bill. Then Left resets; Right drives again with the remaining balance.
